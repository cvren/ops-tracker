## v0.5.0 contract fixed in Phase 0

- Theme: `Operational commitments become actionable exceptions`
- Subtheme: `Problems stop being alerts and start becoming work`
- `CommitmentPolicy` is the official `v0.5.0` commitment model
- `CommitmentPolicy.scopeType` is fixed to `WORKSPACE`, `PROJECT`, and `TEMPLATE`
- `CommitmentPolicy.kind` is fixed to `OVERDUE`, `REVIEW_STALE`, `BLOCKED_STALE`, `UNASSIGNED_STALE`, and `RECURRING_FAILED`
- `CommitmentPolicy` stays bounded to `thresholdMinutes` or `thresholdHours` plus fixed severity and activation fields
- `ExceptionCase` is fixed as the future `v0.5.0` source-of-truth ledger and work object
- `ExceptionCase.status` is fixed to `OPEN`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `SNOOZED`, and `RESOLVED`
- same source, same kind, and same policy must converge to one open case through a stable `fingerprint`
- notifications, Slack, email, and any other delivery are derived from `ExceptionCase`
- `v0.5.0` is not a generic policy builder and not a full incident platform

## Known limits carried into v0.5.0 start

- `/workspace` stays read-only for non-admin roles and membership mutation remains admin-only
- `SKIPPED` is contract-valid and UI-visible, but there is still no manager skip action and tick-suppression skips do not create fresh ledger rows
- Execution history still lives in the templates console and task origin panel; there is no dedicated cross-schedule history screen
- `v0.4.0` still does not include notification fan-out, worker orchestration, advanced RRULE recurrence, holiday handling, or analytics

## Architectural constraints

- Keep `Membership.role` as the single effective workspace role boundary
- Keep `RecurringExecution` as the source-of-truth recurring ledger
- Keep manual generate, scheduled tick, and rerun on one shared execution pipeline
- Keep duplicate safety anchored on `recurringScheduleId + scheduledFor`
- Keep scheduled automation as a single tick entrypoint instead of a worker platform unless a future contract explicitly changes that boundary

## Non-goals carried forward

- full incident platform
- generic automation builder
- policy builder UI
- full multi-channel delivery matrix
- advanced SLA prediction
- org hierarchy
- BI or forecasting
- arbitrary custom rule engine
